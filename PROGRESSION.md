# Progression

Talk to the **Rift Guide** at home (or type `::teleports`) for the teleport menu, in progression order.
Every tier has four monster types, two guardians and a boss. Monsters drop the whole tier's gear rarely; the
first guardian drops helms, shields, gloves and boots; the second drops capes, jewellery, ranged/magic accessories;
the boss drops bodies, legs and weapons. Bosses and guardians can also drop gear two or three tiers higher.

| Tier | Set | Max hit arriving | Max hit in full set | Boss |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 Novice | Verdant | 11 | 16 | Verdant Troll King (296 hp) |
| 2 Apprentice | Jett | 18 | 23 | Jett Wyrm (462 hp) |
| 3 Adept | Tidal | 25 | 32 | Leviathan Demon (647 hp) |
| 4 Toxic | Toxic | 34 | 42 | Toxic Behemoth (869 hp) |
| 5 Veteran | Infernal | 44 | 55 | Infernal King (1124 hp) |
| 6 Elite | Gilded | 56 | 69 | Sunforged Dragon (1424 hp) |
| 7 Royal | Royal | 71 | 85 | Regal Dragon (1775 hp) |
| 8 Champion | Molten | 87 | 105 | Inferno Jad (2182 hp) |
| 9 Mythic | Void | 106 | 127 | Eclipse Dragon (2663 hp) |
| 10 Divine | Rainbow | 128 | 155 | The Rainbow Dragon (3217 hp) |

Max hit uses one formula for melee, ranged and magic: (damage bonus + level) / 6.76. The table assumes these
levels per tier: 40, 55, 70, 85, 99, 110, 120, 130, 140, 150. Skills go to 150.

## Event bosses

One spawns in the event arena 10 minutes after start-up, then every 40 minutes after the last one dies
(announced in chat; `::event` or the menu to get there). They drop 2 mystery boxes, 250K coins, gear from the top
three tiers (1 in 3) and rare overpowered cosmetics (1 in 20).

- Jett Colossus (6000 hp)
- Pumpkin King (6000 hp)
- Frostfather (6000 hp)
- Party Wyrm (6000 hp)
